🚀

Why this hub matters

Saudi Arabia's space sector is accelerating under Vision 2030, with the national space market projected to grow from about USD 8.7 billion in 2024 to around USD 31.6 billion by 2035. Over USD 2.1 billion has already been committed in public funding.

Institutions like the Saudi Space Agency, CST, Supreme Space Council, and the Public Investment Fund now generate frequent calls for proposals, accelerators, and partnership tracks that are difficult to track in one place.

This hub aggregates and tags those opportunities so founders, researchers, and investors can see the evolving pipeline at a glance.

🎯

Who it serves

  • Startups & SMEs looking for grants, tenders, accelerators, equity, and soft loans matched to downstream apps, EO, satcom, and launch-adjacent manufacturing.
  • Public agencies & ministries that want to broadcast problem statements and competitions to attract solution providers.
  • Universities & research labs mapping scholarships, research grants, and training programs such as RDIA calls and the Saudi Space Academy.
  • Investors seeking an overview of sovereign funds, VC platforms, and PIF-backed vehicles that co-invest in space deals.
🔍

Gaps it addresses

  • Fragmented announcement channels across CST, SSA, Monsha'at, RDIA, KAUST, Misk, PIF entities, and international agencies.
  • Inconsistent tracking of open/closed status and recurring deadlines for flagship programs.
  • Ambiguity around who is eligible for global programs requiring European residency or subsidiaries.
  • Limited transparency on ticket sizes, making it hard to prioritize applications.
  • Language and tagging gaps — key schemes described only in Arabic, seldom classified under space-specific sub-sectors.
📊

Methodology & data sources

This hub curates public information from:

  • Official Saudi sources — CST, Saudi Space Agency, RDIA, Monsha'at, Misk, SDAIA, KACST.
  • GCC partners — UAE Space Agency, MBRSC, Oman's OSAP, Qatar Foundation, Bahrain NSSA.
  • Global portals — ESA, EU Funding & Tenders, NASA, international accelerators and funds.
  • Manual research and periodic updates. This is not an official CST or government website.

Dataset updated periodically. Always verify details and check the official link before applying.
